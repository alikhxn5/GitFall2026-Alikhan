One of the latest books I have recently finished reading through is "Cloudmoney" by Brett Scott. The main focus of the book is the ever expanding collaborative world of FinTech (Finance & Technology). Brett delves into the weeds of as to why online banking in general exists in todays modern world. He vouches for its increased efficiency, however draws back when the topic of mass surveilance via data being farmed from the everyday consumer is brought up. He denounces the modern approaches of these banks and tech companies who take advantage of the data they so easily obtain from anyone utilizing their respective services or goods. Thus, in conclusions the guy hates the current world of fintech and hopes that more people become aware of how they are being watched, calculated, and poised by the world around them. He says use cash!
